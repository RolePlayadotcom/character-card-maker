Character Card Checker

Open any AI character card to see what is inside it, check it against the V2 and V3 specs, and convert it. Works with PNG and JSON cards from Janitor AI, Chub, SillyTavern, RisuAI and others, all of which import straight into RolePlaya. Everything happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Made by RolePlaya, the AI roleplay platform for adults, which imports V2 and V3 cards directly. How to import character cards. Make your own card with the Character Card Maker. Open source under the MIT licence.
